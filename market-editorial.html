Good to know

Honest answers, pinned up

Is a "box" a fixed bundle?

No. Everything here adds real individual products to your cart: swap, remove, or change quantities any time before checkout.

What if something sells out?

Availability is checked when you add it. If a product just sold through, we'll tell you exactly what was added and what wasn't.

Do recipes need special equipment?

No. Everything works with ordinary kitchen kit, and each recipe lists the few grocery items it needs beyond what we ship.
